**Description:**

> Dispute Defender reads a seller's PayPal disputes, gathers the evidence (order, payment, tracking at PayPal, carrier scans, store and chat logs), decides whether to defend, offer or refund, and drafts the response with Claude. Every claim is tied to its record, and nothing is sent to PayPal until the seller approves.
>
> Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. PayPal sandbox only. Most footage is the app's Replay mode (recorded, redacted sandbox responses). The approve and the decision (1:02–1:25) were filmed live against PayPal's sandbox, where we trigger the decision with PayPal's sandbox test endpoint; in production PayPal decides.
